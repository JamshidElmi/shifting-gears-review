A look at your website, and where we start

What's already working well

Worth saying up front — this is a healthy foundation, not a rebuild.

  • ✓
    Your tour pages are set up correctly — dates, pricing, and location all show up the way Google expects, which is what lets your tours appear properly in search.
  • ✓
    The site is secure and technically clean — every page loads correctly, nothing broken, nothing misconfigured under the hood.
  • ✓
    It works well on mobile — layout holds up cleanly on a phone, which is where most of your visitors probably are.
  • ✓
    Your homepage copy is genuinely good — clear, specific, written for a real person, not generic filler.
  • ✓
    A 5.0-star rating — this is the hard part. Most businesses never get here. Everything below is about making sure more people find it.

What I'd fix first

Fast, low-effort, and the ones quietly costing you the most right now.

  • 1
    Speed up your homepage
    — it's currently loading a lot of large, uncompressed photos from Instagram. On a phone or slower connection, that can mean a visitor leaves before the page even finishes loading.
  • 2
    Turn on real analytics — right now there's no way to see which tours people are finding through Google, or which pages are actually converting into bookings. This gets that visibility in place.
  • 3
    Clean up old tour listings — a number of past tour dates are still showing up in Google as if they're bookable today. Tidying these up keeps search results pointing to tours you're actually running.

What I'd tackle after that

Cleans up how Google reads and ranks the site long-term.

  • 4
    Give every tour its own description — many pages currently share the same generic search snippet. Each tour should describe itself.
  • 5
    Fix duplicate page titles — several tour dates currently show the same title in search, making them hard to tell apart before clicking.
  • 6
    Connect the site together better — right now very few pages link to each other, and there's no footer with contact info. Both matter for how visitors and Google navigate the site.
  • 7
    Make your business info consistent — your phone number appears in two different formats across the site. Small thing, but it's one of the signals Google uses for local search trust.

Smaller polish

  • +
    Cleaner breadcrumbs in search results — shows a readable path instead of a raw web address.
  • +
    Better-looking shared links — so when a customer shares a tour on Facebook, it looks inviting instead of blank.
  • +
    A general accessibility pass — small fixes like image descriptions that also give a little extra SEO credit.
