Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of bike tours does Shifting Gears offer in Charlottesville, Virginia?

Shifting Gears offers guided bike tours in Charlottesville and Central Virginia, including Virginia's Ecological Winery & Bike Tour, the Flower Farms & Future of Food Tour, and private and custom experiences. Depending on the tour, the day may include wineries, farms, bouquet flower making, vineyards, local food and time learning about agriculture and our food systems.

Can I take a Charlottesville wine tour by bicycle or e-bike?

Yes. Shifting Gears offers a guided winery and bike tour through Virginia wine country, and e-bikes are available for guests who want an extra boost along the way. The winery experience includes two wine tastings, robust locally sourced snacks, vineyard walks, 19 gorgeous miles to be cycled, and a healthy, seasonal lunch.

Do I need to be an experienced cyclist to join a Shifting Gears bike tour?

No. A serious cycling background is not required. In fact, we should have a "No lycra" policy. Shifting Gears' tours are easy-going, and guests have said the experience "works for all levels of riders" and that "you don't have to be an athlete." We like to say that if you are here for the cycling, book a traditional fitness bike, and if you are here for the wine, book an E-bike. Either path is one headed toward fun!

Does Shifting Gears offer private bike tours for weddings, romantic couples experiences, and other groups?

Yes. Shifting Gears offers all of our public tours in a private tour format, as well as custom experiences. We have hosted celebrating couples, birthday parties, wedding weekend groups, and corporate outings.

What makes Shifting Gears different from other Charlottesville wine tours?

This is a whole other level of experience. Shifting Gears, and your guide Jeff, specifically, elevates your average 'winery by bus tour' through bringing a diverse background in grapegrowing and winemaking, wine distribution, and work as a sommelier and wine educator. This translates to you walking away from the day having not just tasted some Virginia wine, but truly understanding what makes Virginia different and where the wine world is headed over the next decade.

You will also fill up on healthy lunches and snacks that are hyper-local to our area – not just snacking on another refrigerated charcuterie plate. Oh, and you'll slow down enough to ride a bicycle through some of the most stunning scenery and place it nicely in your memory bank. Plus, we'll always get some killer photos of you and your group at each major moment. Move beyond the tour bus. Beyond the classic tasting flights. Move your body. Move your mind. Move your soul.
